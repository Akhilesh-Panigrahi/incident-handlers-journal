# Ransomware Incident Analysis

## Incident Overview

This entry documents a simulated ransomware incident involving a healthcare company.

### Incident Classification

- **Incident Type:** Ransomware
- **Organization:** Healthcare company
- **Threat Actor:** Organized group of unethical hackers
- **Initial Access:** Phishing attack
- **Impact:** Critical files encrypted
- **Suspected Motivation:** Financial
- **Initial Response:** Systems were deactivated
- **Response Phase:** Detection and Analysis → Containment, Eradication, and Recovery

---

## The 5 W's

### Who
An organized group of unethical hackers.

### What
A ransomware security incident.

### Where
At a healthcare company.

### When
Tuesday at 9:00 a.m.

### Why
The attackers gained access to the company's systems through a phishing attack and subsequently launched ransomware that encrypted critical files.

The ransom note demanded a large sum of money in exchange for a decryption key, indicating a financial motivation.

---

## Incident Response

The organization initially identified the ransomware incident and requested technical assistance from external organizations.

The organization then took immediate containment action by deactivating its computer systems and collaborated with external organizations to eliminate the ransomware and restore affected systems.

## Investigation Questions

- What preventative measures could reduce the likelihood of a similar incident?

## Analyst Takeaway

The scenario demonstrates how phishing can serve as an initial access vector for ransomware and how incident response progresses from detection and analysis into containment, eradication, and recovery.
